Make the SD card package of the Mass Effect port from your own copy of the Xbox 360 game. Everything runs in this browser tab: your game files never leave your computer.

1 Choose your game

The disc image of the Xbox 360 game. The Russian release has two discs: choose both images (Disc 1 and Disc 2) together, because Feros, Ilos and the ending are complete only across both. It is read in place and never loaded as a whole.

3 DLC packages (optional)

If you own the Xbox 360 downloadable content (Bring Down the Sky, Pinnacle Station), add the package files here (the files with 42-character names from Content/0000000000000000/4D5307E8/00000002/ of an Xbox 360 drive). They are read in place, checked block by block and installed next to the game; their shaders go into the shader package.

or drop it here

    4 Create the package

    First install: the full zip with the game files. Update (a new release): only the program, the settings file and the shaders, to extract over the existing folder. It needs the same disc selected, because the shaders are made from it. The update overwrites masseffect.toml: keep a copy if you changed it.

    Installable NSP (experimental)

    One application to install with DBI or Goldleaf, with the game files, shaders and settings inside. It is encrypted with your own console keys: choose your prod.keys.

    The whole game in one package.

    Nothing is saved on this computer: the page sends the NSP straight to Sphaira on the console.

    A small update for the NSP you installed.

      After it finishes
      
    
      Extract the zip into sdmc:/switch/ on the SD card. You get:
      

      /switch/masseffect-nx/
  masseffect-nx.nro
  masseffect.toml               (settings)
  masseffect_prewarm_list.bin   (pipeline prewarm list of your edition)
  masseffect_shaders.mesp       (the game's shaders, made from your disc)
  masseffect_shaders.mesp.idx   (index of the shader file)
  game_root/                    (the files of your disc)
  masseffect/0000000000000000/  (DLC packages, only if you added any)
      
    
      Download the NSP launcher
      
    
      Install the NSP with your CFW title installer to launch Mass Effect from the HOME menu.
      It uses 39-bit application mode and needs the files above on the SD card.
      You can also launch the NRO with a title override (hold R while starting an installed game).
      
  
      

  
      
    
      Privacy
      
    
      
      The page reads your disc with your browser's own file access, makes the shaders on your computer with WebAssembly and
      writes the zip to your disk. The only network traffic is loading this page and downloading the build of your edition
      (installed as masseffect-nx.nro), masseffect.toml and the pipeline prewarm list of the edition
      (installed as masseffect_prewarm_list.bin) from this site. No game data is uploaded, and there is no tracking.
      Nothing from the game is distributed with the port.
